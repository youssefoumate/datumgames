Dwida: Let Go

Orbital arcade

Slingshot a craft between planets using nothing but gravity and the timing of a single tap. Release too early and you fall; too late and you fly past forever. Dwida is Korean for “to jump” — and the whole game is knowing exactly when to let go.

Dozens of hand-built levels across four worlds, each introducing a new way for orbital mechanics to work against you: unstable orbits, phase gates, repulsors, black holes.

Privacy, plainly

Our games collect as little as possible. Dwida has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no third-party analytics. Your progress stays on your device unless you deliberately turn on the online leaderboard, and even then it is a display name you chose and a completion time — nothing that identifies you.
