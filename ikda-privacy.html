Privacy Policy — Ikda

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Ikda: Korean Reading is a Korean reading app for English speakers. It has no user accounts and no sign-in. Your study progress stays on your device. The app is free and shows advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob; this policy explains what that involves.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are between sessions, Ikda saves the following in your device's own app storage. None of it is ever uploaded — we have no server and cannot see it.

  • Where you are in the course — level, unit and paragraph
  • Which paragraphs you have opened and which you have bookmarked
  • Your review schedule: the words you have met and when each is next due
  • Your streak, gem count and total time spent in the app
  • Your settings — romanization, auto-play, playback speed and daily goal

You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Reset progress, and deleting the app removes it entirely. Your device's operating system may include app data in an Android or iCloud backup if you have backups turned on; that is governed by your account with Google or Apple, not by us.

Advertising

Ikda shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app collects and sends to Google:

DataWhy
Your device's advertising ID To limit how often you see an ad, measure ads and, where you have allowed it, personalise them
IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location is derived To choose ads suitable for your region and to prevent fraud
Device and app information — model, operating system, language, app version To show ads that work on your device
Ad interactions — which ads were shown and whether they were tapped To measure ads and pay for them
Diagnostic information about the ad software To keep ads working and detect abuse

This data is collected by Google, not by us — we receive only aggregated reports such as how many ads were shown. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's privacy policy. Ikda does not send Google anything from your study data above.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Ikda asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can decline, in which case only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device). Deleting it stops it being used for personalised ads in every app.
  • Ad personalisation. You can turn off personalised ads from Google at myadcenter.google.com.

Read aloud

The read-aloud button uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce the Korean lesson text that ships inside the app. On some Android devices that engine is a Google component that may process the text on Google's servers, depending on the engine you have installed and its settings. Ikda does not choose or control this, sends nothing else with it, and the text is only lesson sentences — never anything about you. If this concerns you, choose an offline speech engine in your device's settings or do not use the speaker button.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account
  • No precise location
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own

Children

Ikda is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your study data is deleted with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app. Data collected for ads is held by Google; you can manage or delete it through your Google account and the advertising ID controls above.
