Privacy Policy — Still Running

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Still Running is a single-player story game. It works entirely offline, it has no user accounts, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What the game stores

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your progress and the choices you made in each chapter So the game remembers where you are and how your story goes Stays on your device. Never uploaded.
The names you give the characters So the story can use them Stays on your device. Never uploaded.
Your language setting So the game opens in the language you chose Stays on your device. Never uploaded.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No behavioural profiling of any kind

The game does not connect to any server of ours or anyone else’s. We have no way to see your progress, your choices or the names you type.

Children

Still Running is meant for players aged 13 and over. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the game stores is removed when you uninstall it, or when you clear the app’s storage in your phone’s settings. Because nothing is ever sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, such as purchases or crash reports, this policy will be updated and published at this address before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
