Delete your data — Dwida

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This page explains how to have your data deleted from Dwida, and what is deleted or kept when you do.

How to request deletion

Send an email to ysf.ing.ysf@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, including:

  • The display name exactly as it appears on the leaderboard
  • The approximate date you last submitted a time

Dwida has no accounts and no sign-in, so a display name and rough date are the only way to locate an entry. Without them a specific entry cannot be identified.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. You will receive a reply confirming the deletion.

What is deleted

Everything associated with your leaderboard entry:

DataDeleted
Display nameYes — removed entirely
Level completion times submitted onlineYes — removed entirely
Anonymous ID (random Firebase identifier)Yes — removed entirely

Once deleted, this data is not retained in any form and cannot be recovered.

What is kept

Nothing. Dwida holds no account, email address, phone number, payment details, location or advertising identifier, so there is nothing further to retain after an entry is removed.

Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by Dwida. Those are governed by Google’s privacy policy and are retained by Google for their own legal and accounting obligations.

RevenueCat, which verifies purchases for us, also keeps a record of them under an anonymous ID so they can be restored. If you want that record removed too, say so in your email. Your purchases remain on your Google Play account either way, and “Restore purchase” in the game will recover them.

Deleting data yourself, immediately

You do not need to email anyone to remove data held on your device:

  • In the app: Settings → Progress → Reset all progress
  • Or: uninstall Dwida

Your progress, best times and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. Uninstalling removes them.

Note that this does not remove leaderboard entries already submitted — use the email process above for those.

Avoiding submission entirely

Leaderboard submission is optional and off-able at any time:

Settings → Rankings → Submit times online

With it off, nothing leaves your device at all. The game remains fully playable and you keep every local record.
