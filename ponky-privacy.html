Privacy Policy — Ponky: Monkey Fling

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Ponky is a single-player arcade game: a monkey flung from ride to ride across floating islands. It has no user accounts and no sign-in, and the game itself never sends your progress anywhere. The game is free to play; it shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases, which are handled by Google as described below.

What the game stores on your device

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your best scores, recent runs and the coins you have collected So the game remembers your records and your wallet Stays on your device. Never uploaded by the game.
The nickname you choose Shown on your list of best runs Stays on your device. Never uploaded by the game.
Gadgets you own and have equipped, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, tutorial) So the game is the way you left it Stays on your device. Never uploaded by the game.

Advertising

Ponky shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, device type and settings, and how you interact with the ads. This is collected by Google, not by us; we do not receive it in a form that identifies you. How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google tells the game only what was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No accounts, and no analytics or crash-reporting services of our own

Children

Ponky is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects no personal data from anyone; ads are served as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the game stores is removed when you uninstall it, or when you clear the app’s storage in your phone’s settings. Because the game never sends your progress to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google’s controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Service providers

  • Google AdMob — serves ads. See policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — processes purchases. See policies.google.com/privacy
