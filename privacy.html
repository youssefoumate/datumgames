Privacy Policy — Oberth

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Oberth is a single-player arcade game. It is built to need as little information about you as possible, and it has no user accounts.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you personally. Specifically:

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your progress, best times and settings So the game remembers where you are Stays on your device. Never uploaded.
A display name you choose Shown beside your time on the leaderboard Uploaded only if you leave “Submit times online” on
Your level completion times To rank the leaderboard Same as above
An anonymous ID Distinguishes your leaderboard row from other players Same as above
Crash reports To find and fix bugs Not currently collected — see below

The anonymous ID is generated by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It is a random string. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, device advertising ID, or any account. We cannot use it to identify you, and neither can anyone reading the leaderboard.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party advertising or analytics trackers
  • No behavioural profiling of any kind

Choosing not to take part

Leaderboard submission is a single switch in Settings → Rankings → Submit times online. Turn it off and nothing at all leaves your device. The game is fully playable in this state — you keep every local record.

If you leave the display name blank, your entry appears as “anon”.

Data retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you delete the app, or immediately via Settings → Progress → Reset all progress.

Leaderboard entries are kept so the rankings remain meaningful. To have your entry removed, email the address below with a description of your display name and rough submission time and we will delete it. Full instructions here.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) — hosts leaderboard entries. See Google’s privacy policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Sentry — crash reporting is built into the app but is not enabled, and no crash data is being collected or transmitted. Should that change, this policy will be updated before it takes effect. See sentry.io/privacy

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top will change and the new version will be published at this address before it takes effect.
